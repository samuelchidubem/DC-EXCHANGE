Security center

Your safety comes first. Here's how we protect you, and how you can protect yourself.

How we protect your money

  • 256-bit encryption on all online and mobile sessions
  • Two-factor authentication on sign in
  • 24/7 fraud monitoring with instant alerts
  • Zero liability for unauthorized card transactions reported promptly
  • Deposits insured by the FDIC up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category

Tips to stay safe

  1. Use a unique password for your banking login.
  2. Turn on text or email alerts for large transactions.
  3. Never share one-time codes with anyone, including people claiming to be from the bank.
  4. Be cautious of unexpected emails, texts, and calls asking for personal information.
  5. Sign out when you finish, especially on shared devices.

Report fraud

If you see a charge you don't recognize, call 1-800-555-0123 right away. You can also forward suspicious emails to phishing@westfargobank.example.

Help center

Frequently asked questions

How do I open an account?

Select "Open an account," choose the account type, and complete the short form. You'll need a government-issued ID and your Social Security number.

Is my money safe at West Fargo Bank?

Yes. Eligible deposits are insured by the FDIC up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign in page, select "Forgot password?" and follow the steps. We'll send a one-time code to your phone or email.

How long does a transfer take?

Transfers between your West Fargo Bank accounts are instant. Transfers to external accounts take 1 to 3 business days.

What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?

Lock the card in online banking immediately, then call 1-800-555-0123 to request a replacement.

How do I avoid the monthly fee?

Each checking account has its own fee waiver requirements. See the checking comparison table in Personal banking.

How do I dispute a transaction?

Sign in, open the transaction, and select "Report a problem." You can also call us at 1-800-555-0199.
